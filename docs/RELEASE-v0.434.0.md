# v0.434.0

- Added individual audio equalizer controls with frequency labels and decibel values. The existing presets and equalizer button stay in place.
- Adjusting a preset creates a Custom setting while keeping its other bands and preamp. Switching to Off or another preset preserves your Custom setting; Reset returns Custom to flat.
- Custom equalizer settings are restored on restart in the installed app. The popup supports keyboard adjustment and smaller windows.
